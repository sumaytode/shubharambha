Welfare Fund

Support for Members in Difficult Times

  • From the welfare fund, 3% of the total turnover will be deposited.
  • This fund will be utilized to provide financial assistance to members facing social, natural calamities, and medical emergencies.
  • Members can avail themselves of this financial support to get out of difficult situations.
  • This facility will be available through the trust formed by the Society.
  • Necessary rules and regulations will be decided by the managing board for this purpose.

Sales Performance Bonus

Get Attractive Bonuses on Sales Performance

  • Based on the sales performance, you can earn attractive bonuses.
  • According to the sales value, you will receive 2.90% for one retailer.
  • For 5 retailers, you will receive 2.90%.
  • For 10 retailers, you will receive 2.90%.
  • For 5 crores of sales, you will receive 4.90%.
